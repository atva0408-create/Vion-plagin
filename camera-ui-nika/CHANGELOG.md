## [0.2.0]

- Care: NIKA can ask VION to look after a child with a camera — what the child does, whether they sleep, whether the room is tidy — on a schedule or right now, and hears when the answer changes or an alarm is raised. Reading and «look now» go at once; making, changing and deleting a task wait for a parent, as more screen time does. Needs VION 2.7.0 or newer; on an older server the commands say that the server has to be updated
- An alarm of care reaches NIKA once, with what her scenario needs to act on it, and is not also said as a notification
- The answer to a request a parent allowed brings what was done with it

## [0.1.0]

- First version: VION finds NIKA on the home network and connects her with a code she says aloud; NIKA says VION notifications; she asks VION about the screen time of VOICE and gives more time (minutes, skipping a break, until a time) with a parent's permission on the phone, on this page or in the chat of the assistant; a journal; tools of the assistant for all of it
